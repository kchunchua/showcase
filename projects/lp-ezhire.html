EZHire - ATS Platform

Responsive ATS Platform Landing Page

Role

UX/UI Designer

Tools

Figma, Illustrator

Website

Visit EZHire call_made

Requirement

EZHire needed a responsive landing page that could introduce its recruitment platform clearly and turn interested visitors into qualified leads. The page needed to explain how the Career Page and Applicant Tracking System work together without overwhelming people with product details.

The primary audience included HR teams, recruiters, and growing businesses looking for a simpler way to publish jobs, manage candidates, and collaborate throughout the hiring process.

  • Business goal: Present EZHire as an approachable recruitment platform and encourage visitors to start a conversation or explore the product.
  • Core message: Show that teams can publish jobs, attract candidates, and manage hiring activities from one connected platform.
  • Content hierarchy: Lead with a clear value proposition, then introduce key benefits, explain the workflow, build trust, and finish with a focused call to action.
  • Responsive experience: Keep the message, product visuals, and primary actions easy to understand across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
  • Design delivery: Create reusable sections and components that support future content updates and give developers clear responsive behavior to implement.

Design Thinking Process

I used a design thinking process to connect business requirements with the questions visitors would have when first learning about EZHire. This helped shape the page around understanding and decision-making rather than presenting a long list of features.

01

Empathize

I reviewed the product, existing business materials, stakeholder feedback, and common recruitment challenges. This helped identify what HR teams need to understand before they feel confident exploring a new hiring platform.

02

Define

I translated the research into one clear communication problem: visitors needed to quickly understand what EZHire does, how it improves their hiring workflow, and what action they should take next.

03

Ideate

I explored different content flows and section arrangements, prioritizing a strong hero message, product benefits, a simple explanation of the hiring workflow, supporting proof, and repeated calls to action at useful decision points.

04

Prototype

I created responsive wireframes and high-fidelity designs in Figma, using reusable components and consistent spacing, typography, and image behavior. Interactive prototypes helped communicate how the page would respond across screen sizes.

05

Test and refine

I reviewed the flow with business teams, project managers, stakeholders, and engineers. Their feedback helped simplify the message, strengthen the visual hierarchy, clarify calls to action, and prepare the design for development handoff.

The final direction provides a clear, reusable foundation for presenting EZHire online. The structure can grow as the product evolves while keeping the main story and conversion path easy to follow.
