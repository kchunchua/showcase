About

Good design feels effortless.

I design digital products that are easy to use, thoughtful in the details, and memorable for the right reasons.

How I work

I’m a product designer who enjoys solving complex problems by balancing user needs, business goals, and technical requirements. I work across the entire design process, from research and planning to interface design and final delivery.

I collaborate closely with business teams, project managers, stakeholders, and engineers to align goals, test ideas early, and create practical design solutions that can continue to grow after launch.

My Creative Toolkit

  1. Figma

    Create user flows, wireframes, and polished UI designs, using components, variables, and design systems to keep experiences consistent.

  2. HTML

    Structure web content and bring design concepts to life in the browser.

  3. CSS

    Style and refine web interfaces, including layouts, spacing, typography, and responsive behavior.

  4. AI-Assisted Workflow

    Use AI tools to quickly build interactive prototypes and explore design ideas, including connecting Figma with code.

  5. GitHub Deployment

    Publish AI-generated prototypes through GitHub and manage the files needed to share them online.

Beyond the screen

When I’m away from Figma, I recharge through reading, music, meditation, and exercise. Reading introduces new perspectives, music keeps me attentive to rhythm, meditation strengthens patience and observation, and movement helps me return with clearer focus. These habits shape how I approach UX/UI design—with curiosity, balance, and care for the details that influence how an experience feels.
